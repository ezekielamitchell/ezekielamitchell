### Generation prompt

Use case: style-transfer.
Asset type: wide personal GitHub profile banner, 3:1 aspect ratio, target 1536x512.
Input image: the attached coastal landscape photograph is the edit target.
Transform this exact photograph into a beautifully art-directed pixelated photographic landscape. Preserve its recognisable composition: evergreen branches framing the left foreground, rugged sea stacks and trees in the middle and right, dark sheltered ocean channel, pale Pacific Northwest fog above. Keep the geography and arrangement very close to the original.
Style: restrained low-resolution digital memory / dithered landscape study. Clearly visible crisp square pixels around 6–8 output pixels wide, careful limited tonal palette, fine ordered-dither transitions in the fog and water, larger quiet clusters of pixels in the trees. Preserve photographic depth and natural detail while making the pixelation deliberate, not a blur filter or cartoon. Palette: muted olive/moss green, near-black forest, warm grey stone, bone-coloured mist, a trace of muted copper in natural rock details. Quiet, solitary, grounded and atmospheric.
Own twist: let the mist dissolve gently into a sparse checker-dither at the far upper right, while keeping a strong coherent image; understated archival digital texture.
Edge-to-edge landscape image, no lettering, no words, no numbers, no symbols, no border, no UI, no logos, no invented objects, no neon, no sci-fi HUD, no smooth vector art.
